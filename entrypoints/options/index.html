HTML5 Native Video Controls Workaround

Standard browser <video controls> players scale their built-in controls when CSS transforms are applied to the video element, causing the toolbar to be pushed off-screen at higher zoom levels and breaking in fullscreen due to browser top-layer occlusion. just_zoom provides a native HTML5 workaround that isolates the video in an unscaled Shadow DOM player container with a 1:1 pixel-faithful controls bar:

  • Playback & Timeline: Play/Pause/Replay toggle, precision scrub timeline with buffered progress, and hover timestamp preview.
  • Audio Controls: Upward vertical volume slider with mute toggle and volume persistence.
  • Speed & PiP: 3-dots menu with speed selection (0.25× to 2×) and Picture-in-Picture.
  • Video Download: Native download action in the 3-dots menu for accessible media streams.
  • Fullscreen Fix: Container-level fullscreen that keeps controls and overlays accessible without top-layer clipping.
  • Shortcuts & Auto-Hide: Space/K for play, arrows for scrub/volume, F for fullscreen, M for mute, and cursor auto-suppression on idle.

Generic HTML5 & Other Players

For generic player frameworks (such as Video.js, Plyr, JW Player, Shaka) and streaming sites without dedicated adapters, just_zoom automatically provides the non-intrusive floating popup button. This ensures zoom and framing controls are always readily accessible without risking layout collisions or breaking custom player toolbars.

Ambience Lighting

Ambience currently works on non-DRM protected videos (such as YouTube and standard HTML5 video). Hardware DRM on platforms like Netflix, Disney+, and Prime Video prevents direct frame sampling, so Ambience remains inactive on those streams.

Shadow DOM Video Discovery

Configure how just_zoom scans for video elements nested inside open Shadow DOM roots when no direct document video is detected.

TreeWalker (Recommended): Uses browser-native C++ pointer traversal to find open shadow roots without allocating large JavaScript arrays. Ideal for complex single-page apps.
Brute-Force: Scans every element in the document tree using querySelectorAll('*'). Kept as an experimental fallback in case a non-standard or deeply wrapped Web Component player is not discovered.

HTML5 Native Video Controls Workaround

Replaces standard browser <video controls> with an unscaled, pixel-faithful Shadow DOM controls bar. This prevents player controls from zooming off-screen at high zoom levels and resolves Chromium fullscreen top-layer occlusion bugs.

When enabled (default), standalone HTML5 video elements get clean, unzoomed player controls with full feature parity (timeline scrubber, vertical volume slider, playback speed, Picture-in-Picture, and video download where permitted). When disabled, just_zoom leaves the browser's native controls untouched and falls back to the floating popup button.
